# Business Requirement Document (BRD)

## 1. Business Background
The business wants to understand sales performance across regions, products, and time periods to improve profitability and decision-making.

## 2. Business Objectives
- Identify revenue and profit trends
- Find top and underperforming products
- Analyze regional performance
- Support data-driven decisions

## 3. Key Stakeholders
- Sales Manager
- Finance Team
- Business Head

## 4. Key Business Questions
- What is the total revenue and profit?
- Which region generates the highest revenue?
- Which products are loss-making?
- How is sales growing month-over-month?

## 5. KPIs Defined
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin (%)
- Month-over-Month Growth
- Top 5 Products by Revenue

## 6. Success Criteria
- Accurate KPI calculation
- Easy-to-understand dashboard
- Actionable insights for management
